          Hidden areas

          Crevices and covered surface get fewer triangles, judged by how much of the view from all sides reaches them. Painted areas keep their paint; the Normal detail brush keeps an area out. Turn it off for rooms and other models seen from inside.

          Remeshing

          Rebuilds the surface as quads whose edges follow its shape, and bakes the texture onto new UVs. Paint More or Less detail to change the quad size there; Keep asks for the smallest quads.

          Welding

          Files often store a separate copy of each vertex per triangle corner. Decimators can't collapse those, so they're merged first. Normals further apart than the hard edge angle stay split. Keep the merge distance low: a large one joins separate surfaces that come close, which blocks the reduction and shows as seams.
